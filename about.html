About me

Hi, I’m Harika.

I’m a software engineer who loves traveling, trying new food, and picking up something new. Outside work, I enjoy playing tennis and board games. Splendor and Catan are my favorite board games, and I’ve recently been getting into video games too. I absolutely loved Balatro and to a T.

On my last Europe trip, I got to visit Matterhorn Glacier Paradise and take a pasta-making class in Rome. Those are two memories I keep coming back to. Next on my travel wish list: Japan and New Zealand.

I became a mom in 2026, so life looks a little different these days. I’m still figuring out work and life with a baby, and learning a lot from her along the way. It’s all new to both of us.
