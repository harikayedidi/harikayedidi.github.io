A BIT ABOUT ME

An engineer who cares
about the experience.

I’ve spent more than a decade building web and mobile software. My work has included customer-facing apps, internal tools, and shared component libraries. These days, I’m a Senior UX Engineer at Microsoft, working on the Xbox Design System.

I work through design and code together. That might mean shaping a component API, getting a keyboard interaction right, or building a tool that helps another developer figure out what to do next. I also work on the engineering standards and shared development practices that help teams use and maintain those systems.

My software engineering background is still a big part of how I work. I think about how an interface feels to use, how its pieces fit together, and what it takes to maintain them.

Viome

Senior Front-End Engineer · Web & Mobile

At Viome, a healthcare startup, I worked across both the web and mobile apps and helped redesign the entire app. I built reusable React and React Native components and worked on how we shipped updates.

My work across web, mobile, and the app redesign

Viome is a healthcare startup, and my software engineering role covered both web and mobile. I helped redesign the entire app while also working on the reusable pieces and delivery workflows behind it.

  • I built reusable React and React Native components for the web and mobile apps.
  • I worked closely with product on customer journeys, analytics, and improvements to the experience.
  • I partnered with backend engineers on tools and APIs used by internal and customer-facing applications.
  • I helped modernize the web platform using React, Next.js, and Shopify integrations, and supported internationalization of the website and web app.
  • I led CI/CD automation work and improved the development and deployment workflows so updates were easier to ship.

It was hands-on product engineering: I worked across the app experience, the shared code, and the process of getting changes out to users.

2022 — PRESENT · REDMOND, WA

Microsoft

Senior UX Engineer · Xbox Design System

Microsoft is where I moved into the intersection of design and engineering. I work on the Xbox Design System, turning design ideas into shared components, interactions, and tools that other engineers can use.

My design engineering work at Microsoft

At Microsoft, my work moved into the intersection of design and engineering. I partner with designers and engineers on the Xbox Design System, turning design ideas into components and patterns that other teams can use.

Shared components and cross-platform work

I own and evolve shared component libraries, working on the architecture and reusable patterns behind them. I built and published shared packages in React, React Native, and React Native Windows. I also built a cross-platform demo app for trying and testing components across iOS, Android, Windows, and console, and worked with partner teams to integrate the packages into their apps.

Interaction and accessibility

I work on the details that make shared controls dependable: keyboard and controller input, focus, screen-reader behavior, disabled states, animation lifecycles, and reduced motion. The interaction case study goes into the fixes I made.

AI and developer tooling

I built MCP server infrastructure and AI-assisted agent workflows for design-system support and developer workflows. I also worked on tools for inspecting and validating components, with findings that distinguish issues from missing information.

Engineering practices and delivery

I help define engineering standards, component patterns, and cross-team development practices that keep design and implementation aligned. I also established CI/CD pipelines and automated package release workflows using Azure DevOps.
